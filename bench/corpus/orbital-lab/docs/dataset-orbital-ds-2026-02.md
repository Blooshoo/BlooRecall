# Dataset: orbital-ds-2026-02

Frozen 2026-02-11 by Ingrid Halvorsen. The benchmark reference for all
post-rewrite tracker and farm work. Part of the freezer.

## Contents

`orbital-ds-2026-02` comprises 62 reels drawn from cage A and cage B captures
between 2026-01-05 and 2026-02-08, totaling 9,844,120 frames. Cohort sizes
land between 250,003 and 250,096 frames per the hose's soft cap. The frame
budget for the freeze was the standard 250000; the overflow is one GOP at
most per cohort and is documented in the intake cheat sheet.

## Composition

- 24 crowd reels (crossing density 4–8 subjects) — the hard set.
- 18 two-person dialogue reels — the calibration set.
- 12 rehearsal reels with intermission-like holds — the long-hold set.
- 8 solo calibration shoots (one subject) — kept for the appearance-scoring
  fallback path.

All reels verified: digests match, boundary reconciliation clean, no rig with
an open skew code contributed a reference view.

## Excluded, deliberately

Everything captured during the heat week of 2026-01-12 to 2026-01-16, because
the farm was clock-throttled and we did not want benchmark renders struck
under degraded encode conditions. The gap is visible in the reel id sequence
and is not missing data.

## Rules of use

Cite the dataset id, not "the February cohort", in anything that leaves the
lab. Benchmarks pinned to it re-run in full when any pinned tracker value
changes — partial reruns against `orbital-ds-2026-02` are how the March draft
tables got their confusing mix, and we do not repeat that.
