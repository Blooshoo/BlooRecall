Written 2026-07-05, refreshed 2026-08-31 after the drive quote came back.

is sequenced so the first two items fit inside it.

## Considering, no decision

   expansion pushes past 6 h, the DAC debate reopens).

   there is one bar and video calls drop. Cheap fix, but it is a want,
   not a need, and the list is honest about the difference.

## Explicitly not buying

- A NAS upgrade/replace. `bramble` is five years old and fine.
- More storage "because it is on sale". The capacity note is the
  decision-maker, not prices.
- A color e-ink dashboard for the hall. It would be lovely. It would also
  be a fifth screen to maintain.

## Rules

- Everything on this list is bought from the rack fund first, general
  money second. The fund exists so the household can see what the hobby
  actually costs.
- Nothing ships to "eventually". If it stays on "considering" past two
  quarterly lists, it comes off.
