# Plan — media box relocation (2025 edition)

Status: **superseded by `plans/media-server-relocation-2026.md`**
(2026-06-20). Kept for the record; the 2025 plan was executed and then
partially undone.

## Idea

Move `marquee` (the media box) out of the office and into the
living-room AV cabinet, next to the TV it feeds. Written 2025-09-28,
executed 2025-10-19.

## Motivation

- The office runs hot in September; the box's fan was audible through the
  wall during recordings and transcodes.
- HDMI from the office to the TV ran on a 12-meter passive cable that
  dropped signal whenever anyone wiggled the desk.
- Putting the box next to the TV allowed a 1.5-meter HDMI run and removed
  the whole class of cable problems.

## Plan of record (executed as written)

1. Move `marquee` to the AV cabinet. Shelf below the receiver, feet on
   rubber pads (the cabinet is wood; heat pooling was the known risk).
2. New HDMI: 1.5 m, straight to the TV. Old long cable retired.
3. Networking: wireless for the first month (the cabinet has no ethernet),
   with a wired run to be pulled "eventually".
4. Ventilation: cabinet door left open while in use.

## What actually happened, by 2026-06

- The wireless link was the entire problem. Cabinet + wood + the TV's own
  electronics killed the signal enough that evening sessions hitched, and
  the fix kept being deferred because pulling cable through the wall is a
  weekend with a drill.
- Heat: the cabinet ran noticeably warmer than the office ever did, and
  the box throttled twice during the 2026-01 cold snap when the door was
  closed and the receiver was also running.
- Dust: the cabinet collects it; the office did not.

The 2026 plan reverses the location decision and fixes the cable problem
a different way. Lessons kept: rubber pads (kept), 1.5 m HDMI at the TV
(kept, but fed from the rack now), wireless-into-the-cabinet (never again).
