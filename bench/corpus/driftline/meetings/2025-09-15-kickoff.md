# Kickoff — driftline

2025-09-15, 10:00–12:30, long table. Attendees: Priya Raman, Marisol Vega, Tomás Iriarte, Dev Okonkwo. Notes: Priya.

## What we are making

A 2D momentum platformer on Ridgeline. You are a fox. The whole game is speed you can read: every system should make going fast *legible* — to the player, to a friend watching over their shoulder, to us in a capture. Working name: driftline. Character codename: Ember.

## Pillars (agreed, in order)

1. **Speed you can read.** If a fast moment doesn't look fast, the moment is broken, not the player.
2. **The world never stops you to show itself.** No cutscene-locks, no loading walls inside a route, no menus that interrupt a run.
3. **60 fps or cut it.** A feature that can't hold the frame on the reference box doesn't ship. This is pillar three but it is the one we expect to actually bite.

## Roles

Priya produces and owns the changelog. Marisol owns everything drawn. Tomás owns everything felt (movement, input, capture). Dev owns everything traversed. Ridgeline work is shared, Marisol-first for render, Tomás-first for sim.

## Risks (initial register)

- **Ridgeline 0.8 is young.** The atlas system has never carried a real game. Mitigation: the first milestone is chosen to stress it (lots of pose art).
- **Momentum tuning is famous for eating projects like this.** Mitigation: tuning passes get fixed scopes and written triggers (see the first tuning plan when it exists).
- **Four people, no tools programmer.** Mitigation: hire or promote in 2026; until then, tooling debt gets written down, not ignored.

## First milestone

**0.9.0 by 2025-09-28**: first playable — run, boost pads, friction, the Lowline Quarries blockout start, coyote window v1. Tomás and Dev pair on the blockout. It is two weeks out on purpose: we want the smell of the real thing before anyone falls in love with a plan.
