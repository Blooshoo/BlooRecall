# batch-K-029

**Cycle:** 2026-02-15 to 2026-02-23
**Vessel:** 2.5 L glass jar, on the heat mat at 24 °C
**Brew:** 2.2 L water, 100 g white sugar (45 g/L), 4 plain black tea bags
**Starter:** 250 ml from K-028 plus the pancake
**Harvest:** day 9

## Notes through the cycle

First batch after the strip thermometer went on the glass — no more guessing whether the mat was working. By day 3 the surface smell had turned bready-to-bright; by day 7 a fresh culture layer had fully formed, the thickest of the winter so far at about 8 mm. Harvested day 9 on the smell test, not the calendar, and the taste confirmed: sharp but balanced, dry with a soft round edge, the best of the mat-heated batches to date.

## Bottling

Four 750 ml bottles: two plain, one with ginger coins, one with the last of the December apple slices. All bottles went into the lidded tub — the post-incident standing rule — and none of them needed it, but the June gasket-leaker later proved the tub is not decorative.

## Lessons

- 45 g/L sugar on the 2.5 L vessel lands drier than the autumn batches at 55; winter's slower cycles can take the lower charge.
- The smell-first harvest is now policy; day-count-only harvesting made the autumn 2025 sharp-cider fortnight.
- Tasting note, day 2 in the fridge: ginger bottle perfect, apple bottle quiet — apple wants the 46-hour window, not 60.
