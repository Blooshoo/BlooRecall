# batch-KC-003 — first kimchi on the v2 method

**Date:** packed 2026-05-16, fridge 2026-05-21, first taste 2026-05-26
**Recipe:** recipes/kimchi-baechu-v2.md (dry salt, 2.2%)
**Batch:** 2.2 kg trimmed napa, 48 g coarse non-iodised salt, 45 g chili flakes, 25 ml fish sauce, 1 tbsp rice porridge, 120 g grated apple, garlic and ginger, 250 g radish, 40 g spring onion
**Jars:** two 1 L jars
**Ferment:** 5 days at 20 °C, then the fridge

## How it went

The dry salt did exactly what v2 promised: by hour 4 the cabbage had slumped and given up its own liquid, and after one quick rinse the leaves stayed coated in paste instead of shedding it into a drain. Packed with gloves, pressed until the liquid rose, lids loose. By hour 12 both jars showed bubbles lifting through the brine; day 3 the liquid had gone properly milky (the good kind — guides/milky-brine.md exists because of KC-001's scare). Into the fridge on day 5.

## Tasting

- Day 5 (fridge day): bright, apple-forward, crunch intact. Salt quiet where v1's was loud.
- Day 10: the target profile — chili first, gentle sour second, salt a distant third. The apple reads as roundness, not fruit.
- Week 3: softer but still crunchy at the stalks; older jars graduate into kimchi pancakes, which is the correct retirement plan.

## Verdict

v2 replaces v1 permanently. KC-001 (2025-09-28, wet-brine v1) was salty and pale-pasted; KC-002 (2025-11, also v1) additionally melted above the radiator — both documented in troubleshooting/kimchi-triage.md. This is the batch the method was rewritten for, and it earned the rewrite.
