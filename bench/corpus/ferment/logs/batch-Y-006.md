# batch-Y-006

**Date:** 2026-02-08
**Milk:** 1 L full-cream, heated to 82 °C, cooled to 43 °C
**Culture:** 2 tbsp kept back from Y-005
**Incubation:** 6 hours at 42 °C in the box dehydrator

## What was different

First batch in the dehydrator instead of the oven-light method (recipes/milk-yogurt.md is the old way). The dehydrator holds 42 °C on the nose, no door peeks, no light-bulb politics. The culture went in whisked into a cup of milk first, then back into the pot, same as always.

## Result

Set like custard at 6 hours flat — the oven-light method needed 8 hours for less firmness, and winter kitchen drafts never came near it. Taste: mild, barely tart, creamy, the least sour of the Y series so far. Strained 1 hour for the top half of the jar; the thick half went on breakfast, the loose half became dressing base.

## Lessons

- 42 °C/6 h in the dehydrator beats 40 °C/8 h in the oven for both texture and schedule.
- Y-007's culture is already reserved: 2 tbsp in its own labelled jar BEFORE anyone gets spoons.
- The dehydrator has quietly become a single-purpose yogurt machine; the fruit-leather trays may forgive it eventually.

Y-series context: Y-001 (2025-11-19, shop culture) through Y-005 were oven-light batches; Y-003 was the lost-culture incident that created the reserve-jar rule.
