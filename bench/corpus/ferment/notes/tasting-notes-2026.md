# Tasting notes 2026

Started 2026-06-21 as a single page so I stop hunting through batch logs for "how did it taste". Newest at the top.

## 2026-09-06 — V-011 first bottled bottle

Red wine vinegar, seven months in, strip reads 3.2% acidity. Rounded, cider-adjacent, a soft tannic tail from the wine. Nothing like the harsh shop stuff. Next batch gets 100 ml more mother liquid to speed it up.

## 2026-08-16 — house chili sauce v2, day 14

The roasted garlic comes through as a warm sweetness under the heat; no seed bitterness this time because half the seeds went out. Strip read 3.8 on day 12. v1 tastes grassy and bright next to it — I will keep both in rotation.

## 2026-07-19 — K-049 peach bottles

Three thin peach slices, 46 hours at 22 °C: stone-fruit aroma loud on opening, sweetness almost gone by day three in the fridge. Two days would have been perfect. Written up in logs/batch-K-049.md.

## 2026-06-14 — M-002 young taste

Six months old and already tasting like caramel and soy with real depth — months ahead of schedule, which makes me suspicious of the pantry temperature. Decision recorded in the log: leave it until autumn anyway.

## 2026-05-24 — K-041

Twelve days on the heat mat: the sharpest liquid I have made, nearly cider. Good for a 10% starter cut, too sharp to drink neat.

## 2026-03-21 — S-021 loaf

First loaf with a genuinely open crumb; the trick was dough temperature, not more water. Tasted sweeter than S-014, which I now blame on the cold, dull winter flour.

## 2026-02-08 — Y-006 yogurt

Six hours at 42 °C: custardy, barely tart, exactly the texture I wanted. The dehydrator earns its shelf space for this alone.
