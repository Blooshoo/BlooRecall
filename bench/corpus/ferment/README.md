# The Ferment Shelf

Personal wiki for everything bubbling in my kitchen. Everything here is what I actually did, measured and tasted, in a small flat kitchen with one warm shelf above the kettle and one very cold stone windowsill. Started 2025-09-28 with the first kimchi of the season; the wiki has grown into the record of a full year of jars.

## How I organise things

- `recipes/` — the way I currently make things. When a method changes enough, I write a new version file and leave the old one up (see kimchi-baechu-v1 vs kimchi-baechu-v2, and the ginger bug pages).
- `logs/` — batch logs. Every batch gets an ID: S = sourdough bake, K = kombucha cycle, KC = kimchi, M = miso, V = vinegar, Y = yogurt. Kombucha numbering counts every cycle including the ones I dumped, so K numbers move faster than the calendar.
- `guides/` — longer reference pages, including the two big ones: sourdough-complete-guide and kombucha-year.
- `troubleshooting/` — what went wrong and what fixed it.
- `notes/` — the equipment list, the 2026 tasting notes, and literal fridge notes transcribed before they got washed off.

## House rules

Weights wherever it matters; the 0.1 g scale lives next to the salt. Temperatures are what the strip thermometer on the jar said, not what the room claimed. If a jar frightens me the default is: lid off over the sink, look, smell, decide slowly. When in doubt, compost it.
