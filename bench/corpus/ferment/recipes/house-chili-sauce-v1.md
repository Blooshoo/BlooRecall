# House chili sauce, v1

Written 2026-06-07. The bright, grassy original. v2 (house-chili-sauce-v2.md) is the rounder garlic version — same sauce, two moods.

## Ingredients

- 500 g red chilies: long mild ones plus 20% bird's eye
- 30 g garlic cloves, peeled
- 25 g non-iodised salt (5% of the chili weight)
- 15 ml white vinegar, for the blender
- One 250 ml bottle

## Method

1. Wipe the chilies, trim the stalks, leave the seeds in. Rough chop.
2. Toss chili and garlic with the salt until glossy. Pack into a clean 500 ml jar, pressing so liquid rises over the solids. Lid loose or cloth.
3. Ferment 7 days at 21 °C. Press everything under the brine once a day with a clean fork; it is a quick job and it keeps the top clean.
4. Pour the lot into a blender, add the vinegar, blitz to a coarse texture — 5 or 6 pulses, not a smooth purée. This one is meant to look rustic.
5. Bottle, fridge. Keeps 3 months, and the heat climbs for the first two weeks then sits still.

## Tasting notes

- Day 7: bright, grassy, front-of-tongue heat that fades fast.
- Week 3: the bitterness from the seeds is more noticeable than on day 7. Not fatal, but there.
- Verdict after three batches: good on eggs and noodles; the coarseness means it slides off chips.

## Lessons

- 5% salt made a firm ferment that never wanted to mould, but it also slows the sour down — the sauce reads more "salted" than "lacto" until week 2.
- The seed bitterness is the main reason v2 exists. Same jar, same routine, different numbers.
