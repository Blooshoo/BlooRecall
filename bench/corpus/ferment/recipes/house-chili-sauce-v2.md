# House chili sauce, v2

Written 2026-08-16, after two months of v1. The rounder, garlic-forward version — same sauce, two moods. v1 (house-chili-sauce-v1.md) stays up because it is genuinely a different tasting bottle.

## Ingredients

- 500 g red chilies: long mild ones plus 20% bird's eye
- 30 g garlic cloves, peeled, plus 40 g roasted garlic
- 14 g non-iodised salt (2.8% of the chili weight)
- One 250 ml bottle, pH strip (0–6 range)

## Method

1. Wipe the chilies, trim the stalks. Deseed half of them — this is the single change that killed the bitterness. Rough chop.
2. Toss chili and raw garlic with the salt until glossy. Pack into a clean 500 ml jar, pressing so liquid rises over the solids. Lid loose or cloth.
3. Ferment 12 days at 21 °C. Press everything under the brine once a day with a clean fork; it is a quick job and it keeps the top clean.
4. Pour the lot into a blender, add the roasted garlic, blitz to silky — a full 60 seconds, not pulses. v2 is the smooth one.
5. Strip check before bottling: day 12 read 3.8 here. Bottle, fridge. Keeps 4 months.

## Tasting notes

- Day 12: warm, round heat, the roasted garlic arriving as sweetness underneath. No seed bitterness anywhere.
- Week 3: deepens further; this is the one for roast chicken and rice.
- Side by side with v1: v1 is grassy and bright, v2 is sweet and low. Both jarred within the same week, both from the same 5 kg chili box.

## Lessons

- 2.8% salt ferments faster and sours faster than v1's 5%; the strip is what makes the earlier bottling safe to judge rather than guesswork.
- Roasting the garlic (15 min at 180 °C, skins on, squeezed out) keeps it sweet instead of sharp through the ferment.
