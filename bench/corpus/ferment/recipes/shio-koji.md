# Shio koji

Written 2025-10-18, straight after my first koji run. This is the jar that lives in the fridge door and quietly improves everything it touches.

## Ratio

- 100 g rice koji
- 33 g non-iodised salt
- 130 ml cooled boiled water

That is the whole recipe. It fills one 750 ml jar with room to stir.

## Method

1. Mix salt and water into the koji in the jar, lid on loose, cloth under the lid if yours has no gasket anyway.
2. Once a day: stir with a clean spoon, press the lid back on. On warm days it can smell faintly of sweet chestnuts by day 3 — normal.
3. Ready in 10–14 days at 20–24 °C, when the grains are soft enough to smear and the jar smells faintly of it all by itself.
4. Blend smooth (optional, I prefer it blended) and keep in the fridge. Mine keeps at least 6 months, though the January batch never lasted past March.

## How I use it

- As a marinade at 10% of the food's weight: fish 1 hour, chicken 4 hours, vegetables 30 minutes.
- A teaspoon in dressings in place of salt, plus a rounder, savoury finish.
- Rubbed on salmon skin-side down for 45 minutes, wiped off, grilled — the weekly dinner.

## Notes

- Salt matters: iodised salt visibly slowed my second jar. Non-iodised only.
- The koji rice for the October 2025 jar was grown in the cooler box at 32 °C (see the great koji flood, logs/2026-01-the-great-koji-flood.md, for what NOT to do with the incubation box).
- Batch log: the crock miso (batch-M-002) packs 950 g of koji, so these little jars are rounds on the side, not the main event.
