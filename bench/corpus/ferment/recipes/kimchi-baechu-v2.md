# Baechu kimchi, v2 (dry salt)

**Replaces recipes/kimchi-baechu-v1.md.** Written 2026-05-14. Why the change: the v1 wet brine washed the paste off the leaves and left the batch too salty, and v1's long room ferment gave KC-002 plenty of chances to overheat. First batch on this method: logs/batch-KC-003.md.

## Ingredients

- 1 napa cabbage, about 2.2 kg trimmed
- 48 g coarse non-iodised salt (2.2% of the trimmed weight)
- 45 g Korean-style chili flakes
- 25 ml fish sauce
- 1 tbsp glutinous rice flour
- 120 g apple, grated (pear in summer)
- 5 cloves garlic, 12 g ginger
- 40 g spring onion, 250 g radish cut in bite-size pieces

## Method

1. Quarter the cabbage, then cut across into 4 cm ribbons. Toss with the salt in a big bowl, massaging until you hear squeaking.
2. Leave 4 hours, tossing every 30 minutes. Drain, then rinse once, quickly. The leaves stay coated this time — no long soak.
3. Porridge: simmer the rice flour with 150 ml water, cool.
4. Mix porridge, flakes, fish sauce, apple, garlic, ginger. Wear gloves; fold through the cabbage, spring onion and radish until everything is red.
5. Pack into two 1 L jars, press until liquid rises above the solids, lid loose.
6. 5 days at 20 °C, then straight to the fridge. Best from day 10.

## Tasting and lessons

- KC-003 tasted balanced at day 10: chili first, salt a distant second, apple rounding the back.
- The 2.2% dry salt leaves enough brine within 12 hours; the leaves keep their crunch for weeks.
- Total active time is down to about 50 minutes.

If anything goes wrong (no movement, collapsed leaves), troubleshooting/kimchi-triage.md is the page.
