# Miso base method

Written 2025-10-19, the evening M-002 got packed. This page is the frame; the batch logs hold the numbers of each run.

## The ratio ladder

Salt as a percentage of the total (cooked beans + koji) weight decides how long the miso wants:

| Style | Salt | Time to first taste |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet white (shiro) | 8% | 6–8 weeks |
| Medium, my default | 10% | 4–6 months |
| Full red | 12% | 12 months+ |

Koji between 30% and 40% of total weight. More koji, faster and sweeter; less koji, longer and sharper.

## Process

1. Soak soybeans overnight (300 g per litre of water), then simmer 4–5 hours until a bean crushes to paste between two fingers.
2. Drain, keeping 250 ml of the cooking liquid. Mash the beans while warm — a potato masher, then a food mill if you own one. Smoothness here pays off for a year.
3. Cool to about 35 °C. Koji dies much above 40 °C.
4. Work the koji and salt through the mash with (gloved) hands, splashing in cooking liquid until it holds together like stiff cookie dough — press it into a ball and no crack should appear.
5. Pack into the crock in fistfuls, punching down corners to drive out air. Smooth the top, dust the surface with a spoon of reserved salt, lay parchment on, weight it (1 kg or so), lid on.
6. Pantry, out of sun. First check at one month: liquid should have risen above the parchment (the tamari). If not, the weight is too light.
7. From 3 months, a thin "young taste" off the side is allowed. Full harvest per the ladder above.
