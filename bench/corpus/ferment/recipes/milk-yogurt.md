# Milk yogurt (oven-light method)

Written 2025-11-22, when the Y series started. No gadget needed beyond the oven light; the dehydrator method in logs/batch-Y-006.md is the upgrade that came later.

## Ingredients

- 1 L full-cream milk
- 2 tbsp live yogurt (shop-bought for Y-001, then always kept back from the last batch)

## Method

1. Heat the milk to 82 °C in a pot, stirring so it does not catch. This step is not optional — it changes the protein so the set is thick.
2. Cool to 43 °C (about 40 minutes off the heat; a strip thermometer on the pot beats guessing).
3. Whisk the 2 tbsp culture into a cup of the milk first, then back into the pot. Whisking straight into the big pot leaves islands of unmixed starter.
4. Pour into a clean 1 L jar, lid on. Into the oven with ONLY the light on, door closed, 8 hours. Do not peek; every peek costs 5 °C.
5. Straight to the fridge, 4 hours minimum before eating.

## Notes and numbers

- 8 hours at roughly 40 °C gives a mild, custardy set. Sourer: give it 10.
- For Greek-style, strain 1 hour through cloth; the whey goes into bread dough.
- Always keep 2 tbsp back in its own little jar before anyone eats the batch. I lost the culture once in December 2025 when the last spoon vanished onto porridge; the shop had to re-supply Y-003.
- Batch logs live in the Y series; Y-006 (2026-02-08) is where the dehydrator at 42 °C for 6 hours replaced the oven light.
