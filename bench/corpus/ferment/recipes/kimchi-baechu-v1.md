# Baechu kimchi, v1 (wet-brine)

> Superseded 2026-05-14 by recipes/kimchi-baechu-v2.md. Kept for the record — KC-001 (2025-09-28) and KC-002 were made this way.

Written 2025-09-28, my first real kimchi of the season and the page that started this wiki.

## Ingredients

- 1 large napa cabbage, about 2 kg after trimming
- 180 g coarse non-iodised salt for the brine
- 2 L cool water for the soak
- 60 g Korean-style chili flakes
- 30 ml fish sauce
- 2 tbsp glutinous rice flour for the porridge
- 150 g ripe pear, grated
- 6 cloves garlic, 15 g ginger
- 50 g spring onion, cut into 2 cm lengths
- 50 g radish, julienned

## Method

1. Cut the cabbage lengthwise into quarters, then into rough 4 cm chunks. Dissolve the salt in the water and submerge the cabbage for 8 hours under a plate.
2. Drain and rinse briefly. This is the step that costs flavour: a lot of the paste rinses away with the brine no matter how gently you drain.
3. Porridge: cook the rice flour with 200 ml water until it thickens, cool it.
4. Mix porridge, flakes, fish sauce, pear, garlic, ginger into a paste. Fold through the cabbage with the spring onion and radish.
5. Pack into a 2 L jar, pressing until the brine rises. Lid on loose.
6. 7 days at 18–20 °C, then the fridge.

## Tasting and lessons

- By day 4 it was noticeably salty; drinkable but you notice the salt first, the chili second.
- The soak washed out so much paste that the stalks were barely coated.
- KC-002 (2025-11) sat above the radiator and the leaves collapsed; see troubleshooting/kimchi-triage.md.

The v2 method fixes both problems with dry salting at 2.2% and a shorter, cooler ferment.
