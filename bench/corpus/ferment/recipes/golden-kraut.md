# Golden kraut

Written 2026-06-28, though I have been making this yellow kraut since the autumn of 2025. This is the pantry version — ground turmeric stirred straight in. (The fresh-rhizome twin lives at golden-hued-turmeric-kraut.md; the names are painfully similar, the jars are not.)

## Ingredients

- 1 kg green cabbage, shredded fine
- 20 g non-iodised salt (2%)
- 8 g ground turmeric
- 1 tsp cracked black pepper
- Zest of one orange (about 5 g)

## Method

1. Toss cabbage and salt in a big bowl, wait 10 minutes until it starts to slump, then massage 5 minutes until a puddle forms.
2. Sprinkle the turmeric, pepper and zest over, and toss again until every shred is yellow. This is the moment the tea towel dies; wear the yellow towel.
3. Pack into the 1 L jar a fistful at a time, pressing each layer down until liquid rises over the cabbage. Leave 3 cm headspace.
4. Lid loose, 10 days at 20 °C out of sun. Then the fridge, where it keeps for months.

## Tasting notes

- Day 10: earthy-sour, the turmeric round rather than bitter, the orange zest showing up as a perfume at the finish.
- Day 30 in the fridge: better. The zest fades, the turmeric does not.
- It stains everything: the towel, the chopping board, the jar rim, your hands if you skip gloves.

## Lessons

- Ground turmeric never rehydrates perfectly, so the brine looks slightly gritty for the first week. It settles.
- At 2% this has never grown anything strange on top in six batches; the weights and the brine line do the work (see guides/surface-fuzz-and-film.md for what I do if anything ever appears).
- Do not be tempted to add raisins. I was tempted once, in November 2025. The less said the better.
