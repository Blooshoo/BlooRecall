# Country loaf (the default bake)

Written 2025-11-09. This is the house recipe every S-series bake starts from. The full technique lives in guides/sourdough-complete-guide.md; individual bakes are logged in the S series.

## Ingredients (one loaf, ~1 kg)

- 450 g strong white flour
- 50 g dark rye flour (10%; go up to 20% in winter when the starter is sluggish)
- 360 g water (72% — 76% in summer)
- 100 g ripe levain (20%)
- 10 g salt (2%)

## Timings

1. Evening before: feed the levain 1:2:2 and leave it on the warm shelf overnight.
2. Morning: mix flour and water, rest 1 hour (the autolyse). Add levain, then salt.
3. Bulk: 5–6 hours at 20–21 °C with four sets of stretch-and-folds in the first two hours. End bulk at about a 50% rise, not a double.
4. Preshape, rest 20 minutes, final shape, into the floured banneton.
5. Fridge overnight, 10–14 hours.
6. Bake from cold: Dutch oven, 20 minutes lid-on, then lid-off until dark — 15–20 minutes more.

## Tasting and lessons

- The rye is why the crumb tastes faintly of toasted seeds even though there are none in it.
- Every problem I had in the first three months (dense grey crumb, fat blisters that collapse, a loaf that baked flat) traced back to dough temperature, which this recipe ignored for too long. The fix belongs to the complete guide, not here.
