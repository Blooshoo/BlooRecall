# Ginger bug

> Superseded 2026-03-02 by guides/ginger-bug-v2.md — the every-other-day schedule kept stalling below 20 °C. Kept for the record.

Written 2025-10-12, when the bug sodas started. This is the original schedule as I ran it through the autumn.

## The idea

A jar of ginger, sugar and water that bubbles on its own; a splash of it starts ginger sodas the same way levain starts bread. One jar, fed indefinitely.

## The v1 schedule (what this page describes)

- 250 ml water in a 750 ml jar, cloth on top.
- Every OTHER day: 2 tsp grated ginger (unpeeled, with its dust) and 2 tsp sugar, stirred in.
- Bubbles should appear within a week at 20 °C.

## How it actually went

- Through October 2025 it worked, barely: foam on top by day 6, first soda (lemon-ginger) bottled day 9.
- From mid-November the kitchen dropped under 18 °C and the bug went quiet between feedings. Waiting the extra day meant the culture went hungry every single cycle.
- I compensated with double feeds, which swung it between sugary-still and sharp-cider within a fortnight. Two sodas from that stretch (December 2025) were near-syrup; one tasted of nothing but heat.

## What I did not know then

The every-other-day rhythm was the bug, not the room. v2 moved to small daily feeds at 1:1:1 by weight and the stalling vanished — see guides/ginger-bug-v2.md for the schedule now in use, and the storage routine that keeps a jar alive in the fridge between soda runs.
