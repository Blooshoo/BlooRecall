# Ginger bug v2

**Replaces guides/ginger-bug.md.** Written 2026-03-02, after a winter of stalling on the old every-other-day schedule. v2 is small daily feeds by weight; the bug has not missed a soda batch since.

## The v2 schedule

- Jar: 750 ml, cloth on top, living on the warm shelf above the kettle.
- Daily: stir in 20 g grated ginger (unpeeled) and 20 g sugar, plus a splash of water if the level dropped. That is the 1:1:1 rhythm — 20 g : 20 g : 200 ml working liquid.
- Ready when it foams to the top within hours of a feed, usually day 4–5 from scratch at 24 °C.
- Feeding is ten times a week for a minute. The old schedule failed because every second day was a day of hunger; small and daily beats big and rare at room temperature.

## Using it for soda

1. Bottle 40 ml of strained bug liquid per 750 ml bottle of sweet base (lemon-ginger tea sweetened to taste, or straight juice).
2. Lid on, 24–48 hours at room temperature, then fridge. Open over the sink the first time — see the pomegranate incident in the log for why the tub exists.
3. After drawing from the jar, feed it 1:1:1 and top with water to the shoulder.

## Storage between runs

- Fridge the jar, feed once a week, lid resting not clipped. It goes dormant and smells flat-sweet; that is fine.
- To wake it: two days of daily feeds on the warm shelf and it foams again. The March 2026 revival took exactly two feeds.

## Numbers worth keeping

- A healthy bug doubles its foam within 6 hours of feeding at 24 °C.
- If it smells of nail-polish instead of ginger, it is starving: one 1:1:1 feed now and one in 12 hours fixes it, same principle as the sourdough starter (see troubleshooting/starter-smell-triage.md).
- Do not use bleached, chlorinated water straight from a brand-new filter; the two times I did, day 1 was dead quiet. Tap water rested an hour is fine.
