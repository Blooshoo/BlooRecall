# Kickoff — 2025-09-12

Present: Dot Ferris, Juno Park, Rafa Lindqvist, Cece Marlow. Two hours; whiteboard photo in the shared drive.

## What we are building

An analytics dashboard product: boards of chart tiles, threshold rules that page, and a scheduled Monday summary. The working name "lumen-web" stuck by the end of the meeting.

## Decisions

- Stack: TypeScript front end, a small Go service tier, postgres with partitioned tables for storage (ADR 0002 the following week).
- Private beta end of October 2025; GA target Q1 2026. GA actually landed 2025-10-28, ahead of plan, because beta scope was tight.
- Cece owns the visual language from day one. No default library themes, ever.
- Decision records start immediately (ADR 0001).

## Non-goals for beta

Anything beyond single-team login, a mobile layout, and anything resembling a public API.
